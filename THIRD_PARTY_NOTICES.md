## Expanded skin-photo gallery — September 13

Thirteen additional public-domain/CC0 photographs bring the skin-photo library
to **18 distinct photos**. The full app bundles 40 reference images, including
insects and relevant care/context assets used outside the Explore photo grid.
Each additional image's individual source, creator, reuse declaration, exact
subject, and accessible description are recorded in these notice supplements:

- [Mosquito, bed bug, and chigger photographs](docs/BITE_PHOTO_SOURCES_A.md):
  eight `assets/images/bites/source_a_*.jpg` files, all public domain.
- [Sting reactions and tick attachments](docs/BITE_PHOTO_SOURCES_B.md):
  five `assets/images/bites/source_b_*.jpg` files, public domain or CC0.

These are full-frame photographs, proportionally resized to at most 1400 pixels
and JPEG-compressed. No AI generation, retouching, or synthetic variations are
used. Source markings (a circle and a scale label) are preserved. Image count is
not an independent patient or clinical-case count. Tick-attachment photos are
not presented as Lyme disease or other infection examples.

The tick-removal caption is consistent with the current [CDC tick-removal
instructions](https://www.cdc.gov/ticks/after-a-tick-bite/index.html). Each context
image is assigned explicitly to an appropriate guide or Explore collection;
there is no random stock-photo selection. The same handwashing photograph can
appear in multiple relevant care galleries.

The app may crop nonclinical images responsively at display time. Except for
delivery resizing or compression, no substantive alterations are intended.
Five larger downloaded photographs are resized to a maximum 1600-pixel edge
using `tools/optimize_reference_photos.py`; the full frame is retained and no
generative processing or retouching is used.
Credits and source links are retained for provenance even when attribution is
not required. Inclusion does not imply endorsement by CDC or another creator.
